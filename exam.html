Confidentiality & Conduct

  • The content of this exam is for your personal preparation. Do not share, copy, photograph, or distribute any question, answer, or item.
  • Do not use external resources, second devices, or another person's help during the exam.
  • Once you click Begin Exam, the timer starts and cannot be paused.
  • Closing this tab or window mid-exam is treated as an early submit. Your score is recorded based on what you have answered so far.

How to Navigate

📋
Question Navigator on the right lets you jump to any question. Answered items are highlighted; flagged items have a yellow dot.
⚑
Flag for review marks a question to come back to. The Review screen at the end shows all flagged items.
✕
Right-click an answer to cross it out (strike-through) if you've eliminated it. Right-click again to un-strike.
⏱️
Timer at the top right counts down from your full exam time. Warnings appear at 30, 15, 5, and 1 minute remaining. Auto-submits at 0.
🖱️
Drag-and-drop & Multi-Response questions look different from regular MC. Multi-response asks "Select TWO" (or more). Drag-drop has chips you move into buckets — click a placed chip to send it back to the pool.
🖍️
Highlight key words in the question by clicking and dragging across text. A "Highlight" button appears — click it to mark the text yellow. Click "Clear" on a highlight to remove it.
📝
Scratchpad (top-right button) — pop open a notes pad to work through subnetting math, compare options, or jot down thoughts. Persists across the whole exam.
⌨️
Keyboard shortcuts: press 1–5 to pick an option, F to flag, S to open strike menu, N/P for next/previous, R to open the review screen.
🎯
Submit Exam requires double-confirmation. Anything left blank counts as wrong.
