How this works

  • You will get a randomized set of questions weighted by official CompTIA domains.
  • The timer starts the moment the exam loads. When it hits 0, the exam auto-submits.
  • You can flag questions, jump around, and review answers before submitting.
  • Your final score is reported on the CompTIA 100–900 scale with pass/fail.
  • If your teacher has set up score submission, your score is sent automatically when the exam ends.
